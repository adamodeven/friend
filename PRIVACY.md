# Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Friend ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Friend SMS service.

## 1) What Friend Is

Friend is a text-based accountability and task reminder service. It processes inbound and outbound SMS/MMS messages to help users track tasks, deadlines, and follow-ups.

## 2) Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Phone number(s) used to send and receive messages
- Message content (SMS/MMS text and media you send to Friend)
- Message metadata (timestamps, message IDs, delivery status)
- Task, reminder, and profile information you provide
- Technical/service logs needed for reliability and security

## 3) How We Use Information

We use data to:

- Receive and send SMS/MMS messages
- Create and manage reminders, tasks, and check-ins
- Improve reliability, prevent duplicate processing, and troubleshoot issues
- Maintain security and detect abuse
- Comply with legal and carrier requirements

## 4) SMS Consent and Messaging Disclosures

By opting in, you agree to receive conversational and reminder text messages from Friend.

- Message frequency varies based on your activity and settings.
- Message and data rates may apply.
- Reply `STOP` to opt out at any time.
- Reply `HELP` for help.
- Consent to receive messages is not a condition of purchase.

## 5) Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only as needed to operate the service, such as with:

- Messaging providers (for example, Twilio) to deliver SMS/MMS
- Hosting/infrastructure providers to run the application
- Service providers that support security, monitoring, or storage
- Legal authorities when required by law

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except as required to deliver the messaging service.

## 6) Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. We may delete or anonymize data when no longer needed.

## 7) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8) Your Choices

You can:

- Opt out of SMS by replying `STOP`
- Request help by replying `HELP`
- Request deletion or access to your data by contacting us

## 9) Children's Privacy

Friend is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 10) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the Effective date above.
